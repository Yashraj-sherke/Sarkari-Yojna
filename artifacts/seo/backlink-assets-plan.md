# Backlink Assets Plan

## Strategy
To overtake competitors like sarkariyojana.com, we must acquire high-quality backlinks from news outlets, civic tech NGOs, and educational institutions.

## Linkable Assets (To be built)
1. **The "Scheme Status Tracker" Widget**
   - A free embeddable widget that allows local news sites (Hindi dailies) to show live scheme application deadlines for their state.
   - *Link Value*: Natural context, recurring usage.

2. **State-by-State Benchmark Reports**
   - Publish original data/infographics on "Which State Has the Best Housing Support?" or "Top 5 Schemes for Women Entrepreneurs in 2026."
   - *Link Value*: Journalists and bloggers love citing original research and clean graphics.

3. **Eligibility Calculators**
   - E.g., "PM Awas Yojana Subsidy Calculator."
   - *Link Value*: Highly shareable on WhatsApp and Facebook, leading to organic mentions on forums and Quora.

## Outreach Targets
- **Tier 1**: Civic tech NGOs (e.g., Haqdarshak, CivicDataLab).
- **Tier 2**: Hindi News Portals (Dainik Bhaskar, Aaj Tak local bureaus) - pitch the data reports.
- **Tier 3**: Educational portals for scholarship-related schemes (Buddy4Study, local college bulletin boards).
