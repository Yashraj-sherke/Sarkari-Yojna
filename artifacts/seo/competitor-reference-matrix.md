# Competitor Reference Matrix

| Competitor | Domain | Content Strategy | UX Flaws | Our Opportunity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **govtschemes.in** | Central/States Hub | Extensive categorization by state. Heavy use of data tables. | Cluttered top nav. Moving marquees. | Superior visual hierarchy. Better search interaction. Avoid marquees. |
| **sarkariyojana.com** | Generic SEO | Long-form Hindi content targeted at broad search terms. | Slow mobile loading. Ads interrupt reading flow. | "Fact-first" approach. Provide eligibility directly without fluff. No intrusive ads. |
| **jaagrukbharat.com** | Citizen focused | High quality guides. | Limited coverage compared to raw data dumps. | Expand verified data rapidly while maintaining high standards. |
| **schemekosh.com** | Aggregator | Standard scheme aggregation. | Over-reliance on generic stock photos. | Remove heavy stock photos. Use fast, lightweight category icons. |
| **buddy4study.com** | Niche (Scholarships) | Excellent structured data for students. | Niche specific, doesn't cover all schemes. | Adopt their structured data approach for broader schemes. |

## Key Takeaways
1. Competitors win on breadth, but lose on UX and factual clarity.
2. We must differentiate by providing a "No Fluff" experience - facts, eligibility, documents, and application steps.
3. Mobile performance is the easiest win against competitors bogged down by ads and heavy images.
